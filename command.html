Police-reported incidents (City of Detroit open data) within 150 m, about a block, of each stop, plus TransitWatch rider reports. Route scores compare incidents per stop with other routes, and only rider reports can move a route to high concern. Stops within 60 m of each other, such as transit-center bays, are grouped as one location. DDOT publishes shelter and bench inventory; SMART does not. Police data covers Detroit only, so mostly-suburban routes are not scored.
